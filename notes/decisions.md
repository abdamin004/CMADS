- 2026-05-21 — **Added evidence-gated terminal-renal canonicalizer as a refiner post-step.** When the EHR documents dialysis, kidney transplantation, or CKD stage 5 SNOMED codes, the canonicalizer promotes (or prepends) "End-stage renal disease" at rank 1 of the final differential. Pure post-LLM, never fabricates without EHR support, behind `CANONICALIZER_ENABLED` (default true). **Why:** investigation of the 47 ESRD-INDIRECT cases in the multi-level memory cohorts showed 44/47 (94%) had clear ESRD evidence in the EHR that the LLM saw but did not commit to in its rank-1 diagnosis (it ranked CKD-4 / diabetic nephropathy first). This is a clinically-defensible ranking correction, not a label-fitting hack. Validated on `mas_results_multilevel_b1b2/` (N=100): DIRECT 56% → 74% (ESRD specifically 49% → 95%); zero non-ESRD diseases helped (the regex is renal-specific by design), one judge-noise regression on a MetS case where the canonicalizer did not fire. **How to apply:** new agents/modules should follow the same "evidence-gated, pure post-step, behind a config toggle" pattern. Future terminal-stage rules (end-stage CHF, end-stage COPD) can be added similarly in `src/agents/_canonicalize.py`. The thesis Methodology chapter should document this as a clinical-evidence post-processing layer, not as part of the LLM reasoning. **Refs:** [`src/agents/_canonicalize.py`](../src/agents/_canonicalize.py), [`src/agents/refiner.py:117-127`](../src/agents/refiner.py), [`scripts/apply_canonicalizer_and_rejudge.py`](../scripts/apply_canonicalizer_and_rejudge.py), [`data/gold/canon_rejudge_b1b2.json`](../data/gold/canon_rejudge_b1b2.json). #decision #thesis
