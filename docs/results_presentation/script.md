## Slide 2 — What CMADS is  *[≈ 50 s]*

*System-architecture diagram on the left; six labelled bullets on the right.*

- **7 agents on LangGraph** — typed shared state, not custom orchestration.
- **Stage 1 parallel** — EHR Analyst + Lab Interpreter.
- **Adaptive diagnostic loop** — up to 3 rounds with a confidence threshold.
- **Non-destructive review** — Reviewer challenges; Refiner can override but the original output stays in the trace.
- **Gated treatment planning** — NICE guidelines via Qdrant; DIRECT matches only.
- **4-tier memory subsystem** — Working / Episodic / Semantic / Case-based (BioLORD-2023 embeddings).

**Speaker notes:**
> Seven specialised agents coordinated by LangGraph. The pipeline is
> staged and gated: Stage 1 fans out, then Diagnostic Reasoning's
> three-round critique loop, then a non-destructive Reviewer and
> Refiner, then evaluation, then NICE-guideline treatment on confirmed
> matches only. Memory is a separate four-tier subsystem.

| **Δ (ON − OFF)**                   | — | **−24.1 pp** | **+7.5 pp** | **−6.7 pp** | **+6.0 pp** |

**Speaker notes:**
> Two configurations on the same pipeline. Memory ON improves Found
> rate by seven and a half points and cuts MISS by more than half, but
> DIRECT drops twenty-four points. Translated: the case-based prior
> broadens the differential and pulls more correct diseases into the
> top-five list, but the same prior pulls some confirmed top-one
> matches into the related-but-not-exact bucket. The right framing is
> not "memory failed" — it's that the trade is recall over precision,
> and which one you want depends on what the doctor does next. The
> comparison is descriptive — different patient subsets per arm — not
> a paired test.

## Slide 4 — Doctor dashboard · features overview  *[≈ 60 s]*

*Annotated screenshot of `doctor_console.png` with four numbered callouts.*

1. **Agent workflow inline** — every stage's output one click away,
   rendered as a doctor-readable narrative.
2. **Similar past cases (Tier-4 recall)** — top-K neighbours with their
   evaluator match type; one click switches view.
3. **Treatment safety panel** — drugs, interactions, contraindications,
   plus the planner's assumptions & missing-data warnings.
4. **Reviewer note + persistence** — three-way verdict, free text,
   initials. Persisted to `data/gold/annotations/<uuid>.json`.

URL-driven state: `?r=<set>&p=<uuid>&a=<agent>` makes every view
shareable and refresh-safe.

**Speaker notes:**
> The console turns the run output into something a doctor can review.
> Four features. Every agent output is one click away. Similar past
> cases are surfaced. The treatment panel exposes what the planner
> did not know. And the reviewer can record a verdict that persists —
> the only write surface in the UI.

## Slide 5 — Where CMADS sits against the literature  *[≈ 90 s]*

*Seven comparators as bullet cards in a 2-column grid. Each card has
three lines: what they do · result · what they leave out that CMADS adds.*

- **AMIE (Tu 2024)** — Conversational diagnostic AI; OSCE-style consultations.
  Result: beat PCPs on 28/32 specialist axes (149 cases · 20 PCPs).
  Missing: closed backbone · no doctor UI · no inspectable memory.
- **AgentClinic (Schmidgall 2024)** — Benchmark of LLM agents in simulated clinics.
  Result: Claude-3.5 62.1 % on AgentClinic-MedQA · PCPs 54 % ±28.5.
  Missing: a benchmark of others, not a deployed pipeline · no treatment plan.
- **MedAgents (Tang 2024, ACL)** — Multi-agent CoT for zero-shot medical reasoning.
  Result: GPT-4 86.7 % avg on 9 MCQ benchmarks.
  Missing: MCQ-only · no patient data · no memory · no UI.
- **MDTeamGPT (Chen 2025)** — Self-evolving MDT with CorrectKB + ChainKB.
  Result: 90.1 % on MedQA · 83.9 % on PubMedQA.
  Missing: MCQ-only · no end-to-end pipeline · no clinician interface.
- **TAO (Kim 2025, ICML)** — Tiered Agentic Oversight for healthcare safety.
  Result: +8.2 % on 4/5 safety benchmarks · triage 40 % → 60 % w/ MDs.
  Missing: a safety wrapper · no diagnostic pipeline · no case-based memory.
- **ClinicalLab (Yan 2024)** — Coordinator + Chief Physician across 11 departments.
  Result: within ~5 % of senior physicians on 1,500 real cases.
  Missing: closed-source · no published memory subsystem · no doctor UI.
- **ZODIAC (Zhou 2024)** — 5-agent cardiology + clinician review.
  Result: cardiologist-level on 7/8 metrics (qualitative).
  Missing: single specialty · no memory · no treatment plan · no open backbone.

**Speaker notes:**
> Comparison on multiple axes — not only headline accuracy. Each card
> names what the system does, the strongest published result, and
> what it leaves out that CMADS adds. The point is not "CMADS beats
> them" — we evaluate on different data — it's that each row tells
> the reader where this work sits. AMIE is the closest comparator on
> evaluation shape; AgentClinic is the closest on system shape;
> MedAgents, MDTeamGPT and TAO are multi-agent designs evaluated on
> MCQ or safety; ClinicalLab and ZODIAC are the closest on real-case
> coverage. CMADS sits at the union: end-to-end on real-shape EHR,
> inspectable memory, open backbone, doctor UI.
